Protect the camera capture pipeline against concurrent access, reduce capture warmup latency, lower idle animation to 30fps, and skip redundant sprite pushes.

Purpose: Prevents camera corruption when rapid EPC events spawn overlapping capture tasks, reduces photo latency from ~330ms warmup to ~66ms, and cuts idle CPU usage roughly in half.
Output: Updated camera_manager.h with mutex + reduced warmup, updated main.cpp with 30fps idle and dirty-flag optimization.

Note on display thread safety (WARNING 5): After Plan 02's BLOCKER 2 fix, canvas.pushSprite is only called from loop() context (idle animation and result dequeue).        }
